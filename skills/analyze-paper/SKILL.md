Analyze all uploaded GTU Summer/Winter previous-year question papers and create **one PDF containing unit-wise important questions only**.

**1. Extract & Organize**
- Extract all questions and classify them by unit using the uploaded syllabus.
- Preserve question meaning; group genuinely repeated/similar questions.
- Record marks, exam sessions (S23, W24, etc.), and total frequency.
- Include all questions, even those asked once.
- Do not generate answers or explanations.

**2. Unit-Wise Tables**

Create one section per unit, ordered by question frequency (highest first).

Use this exact table format:

| No. | Question | Marks | Times | Paper(s) Asked | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Priority rules:
- MUST DO: 5+ times
- HIGH: 3–4 times
- MEDIUM: 2 times
- LOW: 1 time
- NEW: Recently introduced topic; add this label when identifiable.

Colors: MUST DO—Red; HIGH—Orange; MEDIUM—Blue; LOW—Gray; NEW—Green.

Count occurrences accurately across all papers. Show all relevant exam sessions and marks. Do not invent missing information.

**3. Final Important Questions**

After all units, add **MOST IMPORTANT QUESTIONS (MUST DO + HIGH)**, containing every question asked at least 3 times, sorted by frequency.

Columns: No. | Unit | Question | Marks | Times | Paper(s) Asked

**4. PDF Format**
- Simple, compact, professional, print-friendly A4 layout.
- Include subject name/code, analysis period, number of papers analyzed, and priority legend.
- Use clear unit headings, readable tables, consistent numbering, and page numbers.
- Avoid unnecessary decoration and lengthy explanations.

**5. Verify & Deliver**
Check question coverage, unit assignments, marks, exam sessions, frequencies, and priorities. Ensure the final important-question list matches the unit tables. Flag unclear unit assignments instead of guessing.

Generate exactly one downloadable PDF named:

`[Subject_Code]_GTU_Unit_Wise_Important_Questions.pdf`

**Final rule:** Analyze the uploaded files, create the actual PDF, and provide it for download. Do not return only text or tables. Do not create answers or additional reports.
